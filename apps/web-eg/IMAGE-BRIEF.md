## USAGE NOTES FOR DALL-E / MIDJOURNEY

1. Always include "Hurghada, Egypt" or "Red Sea, Egypt" in the prompt — avoids generic Middle East AI defaults
2. For architecture: say "Egyptian coastal vernacular, NOT Dubai towers, NOT Maldives bungalows"
3. For people: specify "Egyptian" or "Egyptian and European mixed" for tourism context
4. Inject light: "warm amber golden hour" for exterior / "warm professional interior light" for office
5. Add "--style raw" (Midjourney) or "photorealistic, no AI artifacts" to avoid uncanny results
6. Resolution target: 1600×900px minimum for heroes, 800×600px for gallery
