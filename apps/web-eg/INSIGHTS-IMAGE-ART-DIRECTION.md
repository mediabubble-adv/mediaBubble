# Insights Image Art Direction

This series treats every insights cover as a bespoke editorial photograph, not a generic blog thumbnail. The visual DNA should feel unmistakably MediaBubble: Obsidian Creative Studio calm, premium Hurghada context, crafted business realism, and tactile strategy or production details.

## Core DNA

- Editorial realism over stock-photo posing
- Hurghada / Red Sea / Egyptian coastal context wherever it supports the story
- Deep navy and brand-blue structure, warm amber daylight, controlled yellow accents
- Physical materials whenever possible: reports, moodboards, brochures, wireframes, devices
- No readable text, no visible client logos, no fake software branding
- Each scene should express the article's idea through subject, props, and setting
